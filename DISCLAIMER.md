# Disclaimer

This repository contains an educational phishing investigation written for a cybersecurity portfolio. It documents analysis of one email and one PDF attachment received on 20 October 2026.

- The report's classification is an evidence-based assessment, not a court finding or an official determination by Coinbase, Google, or another organization.
- The report assesses the sample as high-confidence suspected callback phishing. It does not identify a threat actor or prove who controls the sender account, callback number, or truncated wallet address.
- Email authentication results indicate whether the message passed the evaluated domain-authentication checks. They do not prove that a message is trustworthy or that the sender is authorized to represent a brand.
- The original email and attachment, sender address, and direct callback number are not published in this repository.
- No call was made to the displayed number, and no account access or financial impact was verified.
- Hashes identify the analyzed files; they do not independently prove maliciousness.
- Findings may change if new evidence becomes available.

Do not use contact information contained in a suspicious message to verify its legitimacy. Navigate independently to the relevant organization's official website or app.

Brand names are mentioned only to describe the apparent impersonation in the sample. This project is independent and is not endorsed by Coinbase, Google, or any other named organization.
